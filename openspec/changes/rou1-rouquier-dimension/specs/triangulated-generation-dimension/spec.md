# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Defines the numerical dimension of an abstract triangulated category from the
extension steps needed by one object, and records how the invariant behaves
under triangulated functors.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Rouquier dimension measures single-object generation

The library SHALL define the Rouquier dimension of a pretriangulated category
as the infimum, in `ℕ∞`, of the generation times from each singleton object to
the whole category. The definition MUST quantify over individual objects, not
over arbitrary generating object properties. The definition MUST NOT select
or privilege a particular object as attaining the infimum; witness theorems
state existential conclusions separately.

#### Scenario: A finite bound has a single-object witness
- **WHEN** the Rouquier dimension is at most a natural number `n`
- **THEN** some object generates every object of the category in at most `n`
  extension steps

#### Scenario: No object generates in finitely many steps
- **WHEN** every object's generation time to the whole category is `⊤`
- **THEN** the Rouquier dimension is `⊤`

#### Scenario: The infimum does not designate a chosen object
- **WHEN** the category's Rouquier dimension is evaluated
- **THEN** the result is a value and does not package a selected minimizing
  object

### Requirement: Finite Rouquier dimension characterizes strong generation

The library SHALL identify finite Rouquier dimension with the existence of a
single strong triangulated generator, and SHALL derive a classical generator
from any such finite dimension.

#### Scenario: A finite dimension yields both generator notions
- **WHEN** a category has Rouquier dimension different from `⊤`
- **THEN** it has a single strong triangulated generator and a classical
  triangulated generator

#### Scenario: Infinite dimension excludes strong generation
- **WHEN** the category has no single strong triangulated generator
- **THEN** its Rouquier dimension is `⊤`

### Requirement: Zero dimension is generation without extensions

The library SHALL characterize Rouquier dimension zero by the condition that
every object is a retract of a finite binary product of shifts of one object.

#### Scenario: A single object generates at the zeroth stage
- **WHEN** every object lies in the shift, binary-product, and retract closure
  of one object
- **THEN** the category has Rouquier dimension zero

#### Scenario: Positive or infinite dimension has no zeroth-stage witness
- **WHEN** no object has that zeroth-stage closure equal to the whole category
- **THEN** the category's Rouquier dimension is not zero

### Requirement: Extension-step indexing matches Rouquier's convention

The library SHALL document that `n` extension steps correspond to Rouquier's
`⟨G⟩_{n+1}` convention, with no offset in the numerical dimension. The
documentation MUST identify the zeroth envelope as the shift, finite-product,
and retract closure of `G`.

#### Scenario: The zeroth stage has no extension step
- **WHEN** the number of extension steps is zero
- **THEN** the measured stage is `⟨G⟩₁`, consisting of shifts, finite products,
  and retracts of `G`

#### Scenario: The dimension bound uses the same index
- **WHEN** a witness generates through stage `⟨G⟩_{n+1}`
- **THEN** it witnesses Rouquier dimension at most `n`, without adding or
  subtracting one

### Requirement: Triangulated functors do not increase generation time on images

For a triangulated functor, the library SHALL transport membership in each
source envelope stage to membership in the corresponding stage generated by
the essential image property, including objects isomorphic to functor images,
and SHALL derive that generation time does not increase after applying the
functor. The implication MUST be stated in the forward direction; a reverse
inclusion is not guaranteed for a general functor.

#### Scenario: An envelope-stage object is transported
- **WHEN** an object belongs to stage `n` generated by a property in the source
- **THEN** its image belongs to stage `n` generated by the image property in
  the target's essential image property

#### Scenario: Generation time is bounded after applying a functor
- **WHEN** the source property reaches a second property in at most `n` steps
- **THEN** the two image properties are related by at most `n` steps

#### Scenario: The general functor has no reverse-stage guarantee
- **WHEN** a target object in the image-property envelope is not the image of
  any source object in the original envelope
- **THEN** the library makes no reverse envelope-inclusion claim

### Requirement: Rouquier dimension is monotone under essential surjectivity

The library SHALL prove that an essentially surjective triangulated functor
from `C` to `D` gives `dim D ≤ dim C`.

#### Scenario: Every target object is isomorphic to an image
- **WHEN** a single source object generates `C` in at most `n` steps and the
  functor is essentially surjective
- **THEN** its image generates `D` in at most `n` steps and `dim D ≤ n`

#### Scenario: Essential surjectivity is absent
- **WHEN** the functor is not essentially surjective
- **THEN** no Rouquier-dimension comparison for all of `D` is claimed from
  image transport alone

### Requirement: Triangulated equivalences preserve generation invariants

The library SHALL prove equality of Rouquier dimensions and invariance of
generation time under a triangulated equivalence.

#### Scenario: Equivalent triangulated categories have equal dimension
- **WHEN** two categories are related by a triangulated equivalence
- **THEN** their Rouquier dimensions are equal

#### Scenario: Equivalent properties have equal generation time
- **WHEN** two object properties correspond under a triangulated equivalence
- **THEN** their generation times are equal

#### Scenario: Strong generation transfers across an equivalence
- **WHEN** one category has a strong triangulated generator and is triangulated
  equivalent to another category
- **THEN** the other category also has a strong triangulated generator
